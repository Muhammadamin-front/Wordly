# Vocora Design System

## Visual world

Vocora is a warm, editorial language-learning desk: cream paper, espresso ink,
fired-clay actions, and restrained teal markers. The visual voice is a vintage
coffee poster translated into a fast, usable educational app—not a dashboard
or a game.

## Foundations

- Paper: `#F3E6CB`, with `#FAF1DD` and `#FFF8EA` for raised reading surfaces.
- Ink: `#24130C`; coffee brown `#54250F` defines structural depth.
- Action: burnt orange `#B94E28` / rust `#A83A25`.
- Secondary marker: muted teal `#467878` / sage teal `#6C9390`.
- Display type: self-hosted Bebas Neue for headings, level names, and key numbers.
- UI type: self-hosted Manrope for controls, labels, and reading copy.

## Components

- Cards are paper surfaces with restrained 12–18px radii, a defined brown edge,
  and offset depth. Major posters and CTAs use a stronger version of that depth.
- Buttons are tactile: rust primary fill, dark edge, and a short press shadow.
- Labels are compact printed stamps, never candy-colored pill collections.
- Navigation uses paper surfaces and a distinct rust/ink active treatment.
- Teal signals supplementary information; it is not the primary action color.

## Illustration and imagery

The orange cat tutor is a recurring mascot for high-salience moments: the home
poster, success/empty states, challenges, and selected promotions. It is not a
replacement for all instructional content. Human stock imagery is not used in
the home hero.

## Responsive rules

Desktop can pair editorial copy with a poster or asymmetric panel. Mobile
preserves the reading order—headline, short explanation, action, then artwork—
and turns dense card collections into single-column or comfortable two-column
layouts. Controls retain a 44px minimum touch target.
