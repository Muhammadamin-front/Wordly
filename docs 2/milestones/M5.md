# M5 — Games (wave 1) + Statistics (design note)

*Shipped July 2026. Done criteria from `docs/03-roadmap.md`: games measurably
generate review events and stats render from real data.*

## What shipped

**Shared review recording** (`app/services/review.py`): the SRS-schedule +
append-only-log + gamification-reward sequence, extracted from the review endpoint so
**games and flashcards record reviews through the exact same path**. Games are not a
separate silo — every game answer is a real SRS review.

**Six games** (`app/services/games.py` + `/games/{type}` + `/games/answer`), each
drawing from the player's own cards (due and weak first, then random fill):

- **Speed Quiz / Fill the Blank / Listen & Guess** — multiple choice. Fill blanks the
  headword out of its example sentence (falls back to the definition as a clue); Listen
  & Guess uses the **browser's built-in speech synthesis** to speak the word, so it works
  today with no audio files.
- **Typing Race** — show the Uzbek translation, type the English word (normalized match).
- **Word Match** — tap word ↔ translation across two columns.
- **Memory** — concentration grid of word/translation tiles.

Distractor options are drawn from the wider published corpus, so multiple-choice always
fills to 4 even when the learner has few cards. Answers map correctness+speed to a
rating: wrong → `again`, correct & fast (≤6s) → `good`, correct & slow → `hard`.

**Statistics** (`app/services/statistics.py` + `/me/statistics`): card-state
distribution (new/learning/review/mastered), all-time and *mature* accuracy (recall on
already-graduated cards), total reviews, time spent, rating breakdown, reviews-by-day
(30d), weak categories (by lapse rate), and forgotten/mastered word lists — all derived
from the `review_logs` and `cards` already accruing. Heatmap reuses `/me/heatmap`.

**Web**: games hub (6 cards) and a per-game player that prepares all randomness
post-fetch and keeps game components pure (React-compiler-clean); statistics page with a
sequential-hue activity heatmap, a reviews-by-day bar chart, a rating-breakdown bar
reusing the reserved Again/Hard/Good/Easy status colors from the review UI, stat tiles,
and weak/forgotten lists. Dashboard Games card and a Statistics link are now live; header
gains 🎮 and 📊.

## Decisions & tradeoffs

- **Games feed the SRS.** The single most important design choice — playing is
  reviewing. No parallel progress system to keep in sync.
- **Client-side TTS for Listen & Guess** instead of blocking on an audio pipeline; real
  native-speaker audio arrives with the media pipeline (M7-era). The design degrades
  gracefully where `speechSynthesis` is unavailable (the answer is still shown as text).
- **Randomness lives in the parent's async prepare step**, never in render — required by
  the React 19 compiler's purity rules and also correct (stable options per question).
- **Weak-category signal is lapse-rate over the user's cards** — simple and card-based;
  a per-review-log accuracy model can refine it later.
- **Charts are hand-built** (CSS/inline) in the app's own palette rather than a charting
  dependency — light, theme-aware, and consistent with the design system per the dataviz
  guidance (sequential single hue for the heatmap; reserved status colors for ratings).
